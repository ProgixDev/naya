## Hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: Naya website hero, landscape 3:2, full photographic image without typography. Create a new candid documentary photograph of two fictional adult Moroccan women in ONE ordinary pearl-white left-hand-drive compact sedan parked beside a quiet Rabat-inspired residential street. The driver, around forty with dark loosely tied-back hair, wears a plain muted plum cotton shirt; the passenger, around thirty with shoulder-length dark hair, wears a simple cream blouse. Photograph from just outside the OPEN DRIVER-SIDE window at eye level: driver on image right in the front seat, passenger on image left in the rear seat directly behind the driver. The steering wheel is in front of the driver on the vehicle's left side. Engine off, car stationary. The driver turns naturally toward the passenger and they share a brief, restrained smile, not a broad staged advertising grin. No gaze at the camera. Both faces clearly visible in the middle of the frame with comfortable margins for responsive cropping.
Foreground: only the subtle lower window sill and side-door edge, below their bodies; no foreground vehicle or hood. Midground: women, correctly placed front and rear seats, plausible seatbelts, coherent hands and steering wheel. Background: an everyday street glimpsed through the opposite windows, a worn pale plaster facade and some greenery; no identifiable landmark, signage, third person or second vehicle. Clear air. A handheld 35mm-equivalent environmental photograph, moderate depth of field: BOTH women and the immediate interior have useful sharpness; architecture is gently softer, not creamy artificial bokeh. Broad late-afternoon open-sky light through the open window from camera-left, mild warm wall bounce, the car roof naturally shadows the upper cabin. Preserve honest contrast and restrained highlight clipping along a sunlit window edge.
Realism is the priority: ordinary people rather than models; visible but subtle pores, natural under-eye texture, slight facial asymmetry, fine smile lines, a few flyaway hairs, minimal everyday makeup, naturally uneven lip color. Cotton has authentic weave and small seated creases. The modest car interior has textile upholstery, a few tiny lint fibers, gently worn trim and plausible subdued reflections; clean and cared-for but not showroom-perfect. Slight sensor grain in shadows, a casually imperfect framing, natural optical softness at the corners. Warm-neutral color, true skin tones, muted plum and cream, no sepia filter. No skin smoothing, fashion retouching, waxy faces, extreme HDR, unreal gloss, collage, text, logos, watermark, duplicate limbs or overlapping cars. All visible car parts belong to the SAME car. Aim for a believable everyday photograph, not a cinematic fashion campaign.

## Driver recruitment

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: Naya driver recruitment photo, square 1:1, no typography. Create a new candid environmental photograph of one fictional adult Moroccan woman around forty-five standing beside the OPEN DRIVER DOOR of ONE ordinary pearl-white compact sedan parked at a quiet curb on a Rabat-inspired everyday residential street. Her softly waved dark hair is loosely tucked behind one ear with several stray strands. Plain muted plum cotton button-up shirt with casually rolled sleeves and ivory straight-leg trousers. Modest everyday clothing and minimal makeup, normal body proportions, an unposed stance with weight slightly on one leg. Her right hand rests lightly on the upper edge of the open door with five natural fingers; left hand relaxed near her trouser pocket. She glances just beside the lens at the photographer with a small, warm, slightly asymmetric smile, as if a colleague asked for a quick photo before work. Show her from head to just below the knees, face well inside the frame.
Composition: woman occupies left and center, the same car's open door and intact side mirror occupy the right; door sill and asphalt are visible below. Door hinge, A-pillar, mirror mounting, window frame, handle and body panels must form one coherent vehicle. No car hood or second vehicle anywhere between camera and woman. Keep face, hands and car-door contact readable, with generous edge margins for a website crop. Background: slightly weathered cream plaster walls, an ordinary metal gate, a few trees, a modest worn curb and a receding sidewalk; no identifiable landmark, advertisements or extra people. Clear air. A colleague's handheld 50mm-equivalent documentary portrait at eye level, moderate depth, face and blouse sharp, background recognizably a real street, only gently soft.
Lighting: broad open-sky daylight from camera-left, sunlit cream wall adds a subtle warm bounce, realistic mild shadow under chin and on the opposite side of the door, soft contact shadow on dry asphalt. Realism over polish: visible natural pores and fine lines around eyes, ordinary under-eye texture, slight skin-tone variation, subtle peach fuzz and a few flyaway hairs, natural nails and knuckle folds. Do not exaggerate imperfections or add injuries. Shirt has small real creases at elbows, waist and button placket; trousers have seated wrinkles; car has understated dust near the lower door, tiny ordinary scuffs on black mirror trim and coherent street reflections. Restrained camera grain, real highlight falloff, no beauty lighting. Warm-neutral colors, honest skin tone, subdued plum and ivory. Clean composition, lived-in materials. No smoothing, plastic skin, oversized glam smile, fashion retouching, cinematic glow, sterile CGI surfaces, extreme HDR, text, logos, watermark, fake license lettering, extra fingers or foreground car obstruction.
